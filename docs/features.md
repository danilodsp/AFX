# Feature reference

Generated by `docs/gen_features.py` from the extractor signatures and
docstrings. Every feature is called as `AFX.FEATURE_MAP[name](signal, sr,
**parameters)` and returns `{name: array}`; "rows" is the size of the first
axis of that array (1 means one value per frame, shape `(n_frames,)`).


## Time

### `zcr`

Zero-crossing rate: sign changes between consecutive samples divided by the frame length.

Units: crossings per sample. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_zero_crossing_rate`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`, `threshold=1e-10`.

### `variance`

Variance of the samples (population, ddof=0).

Units: amplitude^2. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_variance`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`.

### `entropy`

Shannon entropy (bits) of the amplitude histogram with ``num_bins`` equal-width bins.

Units: bits. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_entropy`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `num_bins=100`.

### `crest_factor`

Peak amplitude over RMS. 0 for an all-zero frame.

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_crest_factor`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`.

### `rms_energy`

Root mean square of the samples.

Units: amplitude. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_rms_energy`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`.

### `kurtosis`

Sample kurtosis m4 / m2^2 (biased moments), minus 3 when ``fisher``.

Units: dimensionless. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_kurtosis`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `fisher=True`.

### `short_time_energy`

Sum of squared samples.

Units: amplitude^2. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_short_time_energy`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`.

### `energy_ratio`

Energy before ``split`` (fraction of the frame length) over energy after it.

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_energy_ratio`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `split=0.5`.

### `sample_entropy`

Sample entropy -ln(A/B) (Richman and Moorman, 2000).

Units: nats. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_sample_entropy`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `m=2`, `r=0.2`, `max_samples=20000`.

### `mobility`

Hjorth mobility, RMS(x') / RMS(x) with x' the first difference.

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_mobility`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`.

### `complexity`

RMS(x'') / RMS(x'), second- over first-difference RMS (the mobility of x').

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_complexity`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`.


## Autocorrelation

### `autocorr_variance`

Variance of the raw autocorrelation over lags 0..max_lag-1.

Units: amplitude^4. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_autocorrelation_variance`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.

### `autocorr_snr`

Peak over mean of the autocorrelation over lags 0..max_lag-1.

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_autocorr_snr`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.

### `autocorr_zero_crossings`

Number of sign changes of the autocorrelation over lags 0..max_lag-1.

Units: count. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_autocorr_zero_crossings`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.

### `autocorr_peak_width`

Lags from the first to the last autocorrelation value at or above half the peak, inclusive.

Units: lags. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_autocorr_peak_width`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.

### `autocorr_decay_rate`

Negative least-squares slope of log|acf| over lags 1..max_lag-1. NaN with fewer than 3 lags.

Units: log units per lag. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_autocorr_decay_rate`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.

### `autocorr_coeffs_deviation`

Standard deviation of the autocorrelation over lags 0..max_lag-1.

Units: amplitude^2. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_autocorr_coeffs_deviation`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.

### `max_peak_autocorr_envelope`

Largest autocorrelation value over lags 1..max_lag-1. NaN when only lag 0 exists.

Units: amplitude^2. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.time_domain.extract_max_peak_autocorr_envelope`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `max_lag=100`.


## Spectral

### `spectral_centroid`

Magnitude-weighted mean frequency in Hz, as librosa.feature.spectral_centroid. 0 for a silent frame.

Units: Hz. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_centroid`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_bandwidth`

Magnitude-weighted standard deviation of frequency around the centroid, in Hz (librosa, p=2).

Units: Hz. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_bandwidth`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_rolloff`

Spectral rolloff: the lowest bin frequency at which the cumulative sum of |X|^power reaches ``roll_percent`` of the frame total.

Units: Hz. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_rolloff`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `roll_percent=0.85`, `center=False`, `power=1.0`.

### `spectral_contrast`

Spectral contrast (Jiang et al., 2002), shape (n_bands + 1, n_frames).

Units: dB. Rows: n_bands + 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_contrast`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `n_bands=6`, `fmin=200.0`, `quantile=0.02`, `center=True`.

### `spectral_entropy`

Shannon entropy in bits of the magnitude spectrum normalized to sum 1.

Units: bits. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_entropy`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_flatness`

Geometric over arithmetic mean of the power spectrum (Wiener entropy).

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_flatness`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_flux`

Euclidean distance between consecutive magnitude spectra, shape (n_frames - 1,).

Units: magnitude. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_flux`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=True`.

### `spectral_skewness`

Third standardized moment of frequency under the magnitude spectrum. 0 for a silent or single-bin frame.

Units: dimensionless. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_skewness`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_slope`

Least-squares slope of 20 log10(|X| + 1e-10) against frequency, in dB per Hz.

Units: dB per Hz. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_slope`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_kurtosis`

Fourth standardized moment of frequency under the magnitude spectrum, minus 3 when ``fisher``.

Units: dimensionless. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_kurtosis`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`, `fisher=True`.

### `spectral_crest`

Largest over mean magnitude of the spectrum, per frame.

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_crest`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.

### `spectral_decrease`

Spectral decrease (Peeters, 2004): sum_k (a_k - a_1) / (k - 1) / sum_k a_k over bins k = 2..K.

Units: per bin. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.frequency_domain.extract_spectral_decrease`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=False`.


## Spectrogram

Chromagram from the STFT power spectrum, shape (12, n_frames), row 0 = C.

Units: normalized energy. Rows: 12. Function: `AFX.extractors.cepstral_features.extract_chroma_stft`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=True`, `tuning=0.0`, `normalize=True`.

### `chroma_cqt`

Chromagram from the STFT-based CQT approximation, shape (12, n_frames).

Units: normalized magnitude. Rows: 12. Function: `AFX.extractors.cepstral_features.extract_chroma_cqt`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `n_bins=84`, `bins_per_octave=12`, `fmin=32.70319566257483`, `center=False`.

### `cqt`

Constant-Q magnitude approximated from the STFT, shape (n_bins, n_frames).

Units: magnitude. Rows: n_bins. Function: `AFX.extractors.cepstral_features.extract_cqt`.

Parameters: `hop_length=512`, `n_bins=84`, `bins_per_octave=12`, `fmin=None`, `frame_size=2048`, `center=True`, `n_fft=None`.

### `melspectrogram`

Mel power spectrogram, shape (n_mels, n_frames).

Units: power. Rows: n_mels. Function: `AFX.extractors.cepstral_features.extract_melspectrogram`.

Parameters: `n_mels=128`, `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `fmin=0.0`, `fmax=None`, `center=True`.

### `tonnetz`

Tonal centroid features (Harte et al., 2006), shape (6, n_frames).

Units: dimensionless. Rows: 6. Function: `AFX.extractors.cepstral_features.extract_tonnetz`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `center=True`, `tuning=0.0`.

### `onset_strength`

Spectral-flux onset strength, shape (n_frames,), as librosa.onset.onset_strength.

Units: dB. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.cepstral_features.extract_onset_strength`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `n_mels=128`, `fmin=0.0`, `fmax=None`, `lag=1`, `max_size=1`.

### `mfcc_delta`

MFCC deltas, shape (n_mfcc, n_frames), key 'mfcc_delta'.

Parameters: `n_mfcc=13`, `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `n_mels=128`, `fmin=0.0`, `fmax=None`, `center=False`, `width=9`.

### `gfcc`

## Harmonic

### `pitch`

Fundamental frequency in Hz per frame by YIN, 0 for unvoiced frames.

Units: Hz (0 = unvoiced). Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.harmonic_features.extract_pitch`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `fmin=50.0`, `fmax=2000.0`, `threshold=0.1`.

### `thd`

Total harmonic distortion sqrt(sum_h A_h^2) / A_1, as a ratio.

Units: ratio. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.harmonic_features.extract_thd`.

Parameters: `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `n_harmonics=4`.

### `hnr`

Harmonics-to-noise ratio in dB per frame, 10 log10(r / (1 - r)).

Units: dB. Rows: 1. Function: `AFX.extractors.harmonic_features.extract_hnr`.

Parameters: `frame_size=2048`, `hop_length=512`, `fmin=50.0`, `fmax=2000.0`.


## Linear prediction

Linear prediction coefficients a_1..a_p per frame, shape (order, n_frames).

Units: dimensionless. Rows: order. Function: `AFX.extractors.linear_prediction.extract_lpc`.

Parameters: `order=12`, `frame_size=None`, `hop_length=None`, `center=False`, `window='hamming'`, `pre_emphasis=0.0`.

### `formants`

Formant frequencies F1..Fn in Hz per frame from LPC roots, shape (n_formants, n_frames).

Units: Hz. Rows: n_formants. Function: `AFX.extractors.linear_prediction.extract_formants`.

Parameters: `n_formants=3`, `frame_size=512`, `hop_length=128`, `center=False`, `order=None`, `pre_emphasis=0.97`, `fmin=90.0`, `max_bandwidth=400.0`.
